Privacy Policy for VIDO

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Data Collection

VIDO uses TikTok Login Kit & Content Posting API to allow authorized creators to post AI-generated video reels directly to their TikTok account (@story_1955). We do not sell or store personal credentials.

2. Data Usage

User access tokens are stored securely in encrypted local storage solely for authenticating video publication requests initiated by the account owner.

3. Data Deletion & Contact

Creators may revoke access at any time via TikTok security settings or by contacting max.hix.prox@gmail.com.
